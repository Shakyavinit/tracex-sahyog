🎓 STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) FOR NEW INVESTIGATING OFFICERS
Follow this mandatory 5-step operational protocol to de-anonymize cryptocurrency fraud funnels and obtain lawful disclosure directives from centralized cryptocurrency exchanges under Section 91 BNSS / CrPC.
01
Reconnaissance & Address Ingestion
Paste the suspect cryptocurrency address into the Suspect Crypto Wallet field. TraceX automatically detects whether the target is EVM (0x...), TRON (T...), or Bitcoin (1.../bc1...). Select the chain or let auto-detect configure the parameters.
💡 Pro Tip: Use pre-loaded benchmark files in the sidebar for rapid simulation during court drills.
02
Multi-Hop Blockchain De-Anonymization
Click Initiate Forensic Trace. TraceX traverses transaction graphs through intermediary hops, de-obfuscating peeling chains, mixing contracts (Tornado Cash), and cross-chain bridge hops (Across, Stargate).
03
Terminal VASP Attribution
Inspect the VASP Attribution panel. TraceX matches the terminal deposit address to regulated exchange hot/warm wallets (Binance, KuCoin, WazirX, CoinDCX) and computes an algorithmic confidence score with heuristic breakdowns.
04
Statutory Legal Directive Synthesis
Switch to Freezing Notice tab. Fill in your Investigating Officer details, Police Station, and FIR Number. Click Synthesize Section 91 Notice to generate a legally vetted preservation order under Section 91 BNSS 2023 / CrPC 1973.
05
MHA SAHYOG Gateway Transmission
Click Dispatch to SAHYOG to open the automated compliance gateway. Review pre-drafted statutory parameters and click Confirm & Route to SAHYOG to trigger lawful nodal preservation across registered exchange compliance desks.
